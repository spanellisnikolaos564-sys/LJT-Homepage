I am Junteng Liu, a first-year Ph.D. candidate (2024-Present) in Computer Science at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), where I am a member of the HKUST NLP Group led by Professor Junxian He, who is my Ph.D. supervisor.

I obtained my B.Eng. degree from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024, where Professor Junxian He also advised me during my undergraduate studies. During my time at SJTU I was awarded the Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship.

My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.

## Research Interests

- LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
- LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability
